Tennis

  • The equipment you will need:
    You will need a few things.
    Including a tennis racket, a tennis ball, a person to play with, a court and potentially shoes.
  • How to play:
    Here's how you get started.
    You grab a racket and then hit the ball over the net with one bounce and don't hit it out of the court.
    You will do a lot better if you have good form.
  • Rules:
    What to do and what not to.
    First you serve make sure you stand outside of the box and hit in the cross court box. Than you play
    out the point and count like 15 love, 30 love, 30 15, 40 15, point.
